# QED publication comparison

The reference checkout is HEPCAT publication commit `aa676a9c198f9b50a682adc2bc98be590b4adb0e`. To regenerate the table, run `python3 export-publication-catalog.py /path/to/publication catalog.tsv --kernel /path/to/WolframKernel`. The 38 independent catalog entries come from `tests/benchmarks/reproduction/100k/catalog/input-expressions.wxf` inside the committed `tests/benchmarks/benchmarks.zip`. Its SHA-256 is `be9bdbce817965e227352301a49e8b96ce501b8cf6240673fce0a70d86ba2339`. Two more entries exchange photon legs 3 and 4 in the catalog's four-point mixed-helicity expressions. The last two evaluate the physical and spurious five-positive-photon formulas from `tests/QED-6-point-construction.nb` (SHA-256 `557691155ce5c706a18aab079942541c92a629233a656fc64078fe9d444b090e`). Each expression retains its sign and model parameters. The scope of each real model parameter is named `PUBLICATION` for use with a separately loaded QED model.

Each row gives the publication Wolfram package's `SpinorExpressionComplexity` and Mathematica's `LeafCount` for the complete expression. The former weights momentum insertions, products and denominator structure. The latter measures the syntax tree, including the representation of model parameters. A different factorization of the same numerator can change the two measures in different directions.

The publication's own `drafts/QED-paper/amplitude-review.md` and `amplitude-validation.json` (under `EarlierFormulaReview.FinalMixedExpressions`) record earlier, independently worked improvements. For the three-photon mixed families those targets are 62 physical and 63 spurious (349 leaves in either form). For four photons with one opposite helicity they are 327 physical (1,232 leaves for +++-, 1,220 for +---) and 416 spurious (1,458 leaves in both cases). For the balanced four-photon case the working spurious form agrees with the 712 and 2,311 leaves in the catalog. These are separate complexity targets. The table preserves exactly the signed formulas from the checked-in publication validation campaign.
