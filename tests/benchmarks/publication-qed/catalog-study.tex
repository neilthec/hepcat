\subsection{Coverage of the independent checks through nine points}
\label{sec:extended-validation}
The number of tests depends on which quantity is compared. A signed Feynman
test can detect an incorrect relative phase between amplitudes at fixed external
spins. CalcHEP supplies the complete spin-summed rate. MadGraph can also fix
the photon helicities and sum only over massive spins. We count draws once
per comparison and distinguish a new momentum from a second calculation at the
same momentum.

At nine points the 20 native constructions additionally passed 77,376 sampled
physical-residue tests. Of two million signed Feynman comparisons, 115 triggered
a 160-bit replay. The largest discrepancy before replay was $8.38\times10^{-7}$
and the largest among the replays was $6.17\times10^{-46}$. The separate final-reduction
test triggered 111 replays in two million comparisons. For the direct MadGraph check
of all labeled manuscript formulas, the largest discrepancies were $3.81\times10^{-13}$
(physical) and $5.13\times10^{-12}$ (spurious). The million-draw test used the
subset-current evaluator. The direct test independently exercises the printed
permutation sums.
